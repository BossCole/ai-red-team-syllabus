⚠️ Important Caveats

Privacy: During the free period, your prompts and completions may be used to improve the model (explicitly true for Big Pickle and every other free model). Do not paste secrets, passwords, or confidential code while on the free tier.
NVIDIA trial models (Nemotron 3 Ultra / 3.5 Lightning): Trial use only. Do not submit personal or confidential data — sessions are logged for security and product-improvement purposes.
Muse Spark Contributor Free: Heavily discounted in exchange for permission to use your prompts/completions to train Meta models.
Free ≠ best: Free models generally lack vision and struggle with complex multi-file or long-conversation work. If opencode feels "dumb," upgrading to a paid model often fixes it.

🔁 How to Switch Models

In the opencode terminal, type /models and pick one:
• opencode/big-pickle — quick Q&A (default)
• opencode/mimo-v2.5-free — best free coding work (recommended default for course)
• opencode/nemotron-3-ultra-free — huge documents / whole repo
• opencode/ling-3.0-flash-fin-free — finance & numbers
• opencode/nemotron-3.5-lightning-free — fast throwaway tasks
• opencode/muse-spark-1.2-contributor-free — Meta contributor experiments
